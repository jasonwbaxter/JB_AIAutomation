Jason Baxter’s Microsoft AI Field Guide · No. 9 · Training: business users

Seven work habits to swap for AI ones

A short, hands-on plan for everyday Copilot users. Each session swaps one old habit for the AI way of working, with a 10-minute exercise and a clear finish line.

Audience: Business users with a Copilot licenceFormat: 7 sessions × 30 minTotal: 3.5 hours over 2–4 weeks

Before you start

  • Copilot licence assigned and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app installed
  • Files moved into OneDrive, Teams or SharePoint
  • One real task per person to practise on

How to run it

  • Live session, then practise on real work the same day
  • Champions in each team answer questions
  • Share the best prompts and skills in a Teams channel

How you’ll know it worked

  • People use Copilot most days, not once a week
  • Each person names one agent or skill they rely on
  • Survey: time saved per week, before and after

Session 1

Apps, agents and tool connections

30 min
Old way
Install an app, learn its menus, and click through it yourself.
→
New way
Add an agent from the Agent Store, or connect a tool so Copilot can use it for you.
AppYou open it and do the work
→
AgentDoes a job for you: Researcher, Analyst, a team agent
→
MCP connectionA plug that lets an agent use another system, like a ticketing or CRM tool

Why it matters

An app waits for your clicks. An agent does the task. An MCP connection gives an agent safe access to another system, so it can look things up or take action there.

Try it · 10 min

Open the Agent Store in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app. Add one agent that fits your job and ask it to do one real task.

✓ Done when

  • You can name one agent you use weekly
  • You can say what it connects to and what it can’t see

Session 2

From filing cabinet to digital structure

30 min
Old way
Folders eight levels deep on your desktop, files called final_v3_REAL.docx, and attachments in email.
→
New way
Your files in OneDrive, team files in Teams and SharePoint, clear names, one version, and links instead of attachments.
OneDriveMy drafts and working files
Teams and SharePointTeam files, one version everyone edits
Clear namesProject – topic – date, never “final2”

Why it matters

Copilot only finds what lives in Microsoft 365 and what you have access to. Good names and the right location mean better, faster answers. A copy on your desktop is invisible to Copilot.

Try it · 10 min

Move one live project into a Teams channel. Then ask Copilot: “Summarise the latest on project X and list open decisions.”

✓ Done when

  • Copilot finds your project file when you describe it
  • You share a link, not an attachment

Session 3

Print, export, Markdown, then any format

30 min
Old way
Print to paper or PDF, email it, and retype it when someone needs it in another format.
→
New way
Keep the source live and share a link. Ask Copilot for Markdown, then turn it into a document, deck, email or page.
MarkdownPlain text with headings and lists that AI reads and writes cleanly
→
Word or PDFReport or handout
PowerPointDeck from the same outline
Email or Teams postShort version for the team

Why it matters

A printout is a dead end. Markdown keeps the structure (headings, lists, tables), so the same content converts to any format without retyping or broken layouts.

Try it · 10 min

Ask Copilot for a meeting summary in Markdown. Paste it into Word, then ask Copilot to turn the same outline into a three-slide deck.

✓ Done when

  • One piece of content reused in three formats
  • Nothing retyped by hand

Session 4

From moving the mouse to computer use

30 min
Old way
Click through the same web portal every week to file expenses, copy data or download reports.
→
New way
Describe the task to Cowork. It works in a hidden Edge tab, asks before sensitive steps, and hands back sign-ins to you.

Why it matters

Agents can now operate websites for you, with your own access and nothing more. You stay in charge: you sign in, you approve anything that submits data, and you check the result.

Note: Your admin turns on Cowork browser tasks. They run in Cowork on the web in Microsoft Edge, not on mobile.

Try it · 10 min

Ask Cowork to gather information from a site you use every week and put it in a table. Watch the steps, approve them, and check the output.

✓ Done when

  • One repetitive click task handed off
  • You checked the result against the source

Session 5

From SOP documents to skills and agents

30 min
Old way
A standard operating procedure in a PDF that nobody reads, followed differently by every person.
→
New way
Turn the SOP into a Cowork skill, or an agent with the SOP as its knowledge, so the AI follows the same steps every time.
SOPSteps written for people
→
SkillSteps the AI follows, saved as a SKILL.md file
→
AgentA skill plus knowledge and tools, shared with the team

Why it matters

A skill is an SOP the AI can follow. It runs the same way for everyone, and Cowork checks its quality before you rely on it. Share it, and the whole team works to one standard.

Try it · 10 min

Ask Cowork: “Help me create a skill from this SOP” and attach your most-used procedure. Run it on a real case.

✓ Done when

  • The skill passes Cowork’s quality check
  • A colleague runs it and gets the same result

Session 6

From reading every page to chatting with the document

30 min
Old way
Read 40 pages, highlight, take notes, and compare two versions side by side by hand.
→
New way
Ask the document: summary, risks, what it asks of you. Compare up to five files in OneDrive. Click the citations.

Why it matters

Copilot answers from the file and shows where each answer came from. You still verify anything that matters, but you start from the answer instead of page one.

Try it · 10 min

Open a contract or policy and ask: “What are the renewal and termination terms?” Click each citation to check it.

✓ Done when

  • Two answers checked against the source
  • You know when to read the full text yourself

Session 7

From writing from scratch to editing with Copilot

30 min
Old way
A blank page, an hour of drafting, and edits by email.
→
New way
Draft from your own files, then ask Copilot to edit the document directly. Every change is tracked and you decide.

Why it matters

You become the editor. Copilot drafts from your emails, files and meetings, restructures on request, and tracks every change so you can accept or undo it.

Try it · 10 min

In Word, draft a proposal from two of your files. Ask: “Reorganise into Problem, Options, Recommendation.” Review the tracked changes.

✓ Done when

  • First draft in under 15 minutes
  • Every change reviewed before it’s shared
