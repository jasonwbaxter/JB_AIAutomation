How to pick the right AI model in Copilot

Copilot gives you a choice of models. Tap one to see where you’ll find it, when it fits, when to pick another, and whose data terms apply.

Quick answers — I want to…

1Start on  Auto
2Match the model to the job: speed or depth
3Check data terms before sensitive work
Data terms: Built in Microsoft-hosted Subprocessor (Microsoft terms apply) Independent (provider’s terms apply)
Model names and availability follow Microsoft Learn as of October 2026 and change often. What you see depends on your licence, region and admin settings: Claude is off by default in the EU Data Boundary and UK, xAI and Mistral need admin opt-in, and Frontier models need Frontier enrolment. Cowork model use consumes Copilot Credits.
