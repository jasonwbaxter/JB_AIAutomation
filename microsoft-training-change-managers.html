Jason Baxter’s Microsoft AI Field Guide · No. 11 · Training: change managers

It’s not a training plan. It’s a work redesign.

A 90-day plan for change managers. Training alone doesn’t change how work gets done: you also redesign the work, rebuild the processes, measure real use, and create the roles of the future.

Audience: Change managers, HR and business leadsFormat: 6 sessions × 45 minTotal: 4.5 hours across a 90-day programme

Before you start

  • An executive sponsor and an AI council
  • Three to five priority scenarios with owners
  • A usage baseline before rollout

How to run it

  • Days 1–30: get ready and onboard
  • Days 31–60: deliver impact in priority scenarios
  • Days 61–90: extend, optimise and update roles

How you’ll know it worked

  • Habit, not access: weekly active use rises
  • Each scenario shows hours saved against credits spent
  • Champions and agent owners in every team

Session 1

Why training alone fails

45 min
Old way
Send people on a course, tick the box, and expect work to change.
→
New way
Combine four things: reimagine the work, re-engineer the process, analyse usage, and create the jobs of the future.
ReimagineStart from the outcome, not the old steps
Re-engineerRebuild processes with skills and agents
Analyse usageMeasure habits and value, not licences
Jobs of the futureNew roles, skills and career paths

Why it matters

People adopt what they see their leaders use and what makes their own day easier. Use ADKAR as the people side: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement. Training only covers Knowledge.

Try it · 10 min

With your sponsor, rank three scenarios by value and ease. Write one success measure for each.

✓ Done when

  • Sponsor and AI council named
  • Three scenarios ranked, each with a measure

Session 2

Reimagine the work

45 min
Old way
Add AI to each existing step and call it transformation.
→
New way
Start from the outcome. Ask what the role does when Copilot or an agent produces the first draft, the research or the follow-up.

Why it matters

Speeding up an old process gives small gains. Redesigning it around AI removes whole steps and frees time for judgement, customers and creativity.

Try it · 10 min

Run a one-hour workshop on one process. Mark each step: human, Copilot or agent. Remove the steps nobody needs.

✓ Done when

  • One process redesigned with a named owner
  • Before and after steps documented

Session 3

Re-engineer: SOPs into skills and agents

45 min
Old way
Procedures on a shelf, updated once a year, followed differently by every team.
→
New way
Process owners turn their top SOPs into skills and agents, with human checkpoints and IT governance.

Why it matters

A skill makes the best way of working the default way. Human checkpoints keep quality and accountability. IT keeps agents safe and governed (see No. 10).

Try it · 10 min

List your five highest-volume SOPs. Pilot one as a Cowork skill with a checkpoint before anything is sent or submitted.

✓ Done when

  • One skill in daily use
  • Time saved per run measured

Session 4

Analyse usage, not licences

45 min
Old way
Report how many licences you assigned.
→
New way
Track active use, habit formation and power users in the Copilot Dashboard, then add surveys and listening sessions.

Why it matters

Assigned licences tell you nothing about value. Usage patterns show which teams formed a habit, where people are stuck, and who your champions should be.

Try it · 10 min

Take a baseline now. Review at 30, 60 and 90 days. For the three lowest-use teams, ask them why and fix the top blocker.

✓ Done when

  • A monthly adoption report goes to sponsors
  • Each low-use team has an action

Session 5

Value against cost

45 min
Old way
“AI is free once we have the licence.”
→
New way
Track value per scenario against the credits it uses, and teach people to pick the right model for the job.

Why it matters

Usage-based agents and deep models cost money per use. Leaders keep funding AI when each scenario shows hours saved, quality gained and what it cost.

Try it · 10 min

Add two columns to your scenario scorecard: hours saved per month and credits spent per month. Review them with IT and finance.

✓ Done when

  • Every scenario shows value and cost
  • Model-choice tips are in the user training

Session 6

Create the jobs of the future

45 min
Old way
The same job descriptions, with “uses Copilot” added at the bottom.
→
New way
New responsibilities and roles: agent owners, skill authors, AI champions, process designers and data stewards, with career paths to match.
Agent ownerAccountable for what an agent does
Skill authorTurns know-how into reusable skills
AI championCoaches the team, shares what works
Data stewardKeeps the gold data trusted

Why it matters

When agents take on tasks, people move to directing, checking and improving the work. Name these roles so the work has owners and people can see a future for themselves.

Try it · 10 min

Update two job descriptions with AI responsibilities. Launch a champions network with one champion per team.

✓ Done when

  • Champions in every team
  • Agent owners named for every shared agent
