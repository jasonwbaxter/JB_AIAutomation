iPad, Cloud PC or Copilot+ PC? Choose by where the AI runs

Copilot works on all three. The device decides where the AI runs — in the cloud, on a Cloud PC, or on the device itself — and which extras you get.

Choose an iPad when…

People are mobile — in meetings, on site or travelling — and mostly read, review, annotate and approve. Long battery and pen.

Skip it for heavy modelling or Windows-only apps.

Choose Windows 365 when…

You need a secure, managed Windows desktop on any device — contractors, BYOD, fast onboarding — with data kept in the cloud. Also gives AI agents a desktop to work in.

Skip it for offline work.

Choose a Copilot+ PC when…

Power users want the fastest AI on the device — Click to Do, smarter search, live translated captions — working offline and privately.

Skip it when data must never sit on the endpoint.
